## 3. Configurator Guardrail Rules
- Unit Conversion Prompt: When switching between `ft`, `in`, `cm`, and `mm`, clearly show calculated `sq. ft.` area.
- Tiny Outdoor Sign Nudge: If category is `acp`, `glow`, or `flex` and calculated area is `< 2 sq. ft.`, show a 1-tap button to switch from `Inches` to `Feet`.
- Estimate vs. Confirmed Quote Copy: Always state that printing, selected substrate, and standard finishing are included in the Instant Estimate, while site installation, structural iron framing, artwork setup, and transport are confirmed in the official written quotation.
